- Which method did you use to fill missing values?
We used a rolling mean with 3 days imputation method with forward fill. This coputes the mean of the current day and the preceding 2 days(capturing short term changes) while filling the values with those before measured.
- Why did you choose that method? Because captures short-term flactuations and is more realistic. As shown in the example (at the end), all missing valued are filled,and local treands are preserved. Thus, this method is 
sutable for patient vital sign time series.
- What are the pros/cons of your approach?
Pros: This approach is able to capture local trends rather than simply carrying forward the last observation, reducing noise and increasing the efficiency of our data. 
Limits: This approach is not able to handle extremely long gaps only with rolling mean.
